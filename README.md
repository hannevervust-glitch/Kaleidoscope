# Kaleidoscope

A morphing, interactive kaleidoscope that grows and changes over time.

**Live:** https://hannevervust-glitch.github.io/Kaleidoscope/

## How to use

- **Click** the pattern to send out a burst of energy
- **Space** switches between Wave, Spiral and Explode
- **P** pauses, **H** hides the options panel
- The panel lets you change mirrors, speed, size, density, colour palette, shapes, light trails, energy rings and rotation. Your settings are remembered in your browser.
